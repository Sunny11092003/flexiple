Prototype scope: the Truth Ledger, City Compare and State Policies hold sourced figures from published reports (Nasscom-Zinnov, JLL, Cushman & Wakefield, Colliers, ICRA, CBRE, state policy documents). In production each field is filled by a pipeline of registry filings, lease records, press and policy notifications, and each value keeps its source and date.
